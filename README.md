# UnRecon

A mod that adds a promotion path for Scouts with new promotions to make things more interesting.

Innate: Gain xp when discovering resources (bonus < luxury < strategic), ancient ruins, natural wonders, and when ending their turn in hostile territory. 
Innate: Transfer movement to civilian units. Great for escorting civilians Settlers.
Cartographer: Gain Science when discovering Mountains or Coasts, and Culture from Natural Wonders.
Trailblazer: Build roads (and faster than workers).

Spotter 1-2: Scouts increase the strength of adjacent Ranged and Siege units, and reveal invisible land units (see Ghost)
Forester, Mountainer, Nomad, and Mariner: improve movement and combat in Forest/Jungle, Hills, Desert/Tundra/Snow and Coast/Rivers respectively.
Outrider 1-3: Pillaging Cost no Movement, Ignores Zone of Control, Moves after attacking, Flanking bonuses, and Combat Penalties for enemies.
Ghost: Ignore Open Borders, Become invisible in Rough Terrain, Improvements and Pillaged Improvements.

Promotion Path:
Scout --> Javelin (Bronze Working) --> Skirmisher (Machinery) --> Ranger (Rifling) --> Commando (Flight) --> Infiltrator (Radar) --> Operative (Nanotechnology)
